any task. In short: find code through `Docs/ai/codemap/INDEX.md`, plan and wait for approval before

saying "done", and record every change in `Docs/Thesis/FORK_CHANGES.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

the original app's release process. They were removed for this fork on 2026-10-22.
