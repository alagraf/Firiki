# Firiki changelog

Changes made in this fork of OpenIntelligence (thesis proof of concept). The full record, with the
reason and verification of each change, is in `Docs/Thesis/FORK_CHANGES.md`.


### Added
- **[Legal]** **A Greek legal opinion drawn from several documents of a library.** For a question that needs more than one law, passages are retrieved from each document separately, the documents closest to the question are kept, and where a later law amends or adds an article of the Labour Code the opinion says so and reads the article from the amending law. The on-device model drafts one section a document and a conclusion; every sentence whose figure or article the passages don't support is dropped, and the opinion is translated to Greek under the headings Ερώτημα, Εφαρμοστέο δίκαιο, Τι προβλέπει κάθε κείμενο, Πώς συνδυάζονται, Συμπέρασμα. Development set: 10 of 12 citations, 11 of 20 key facts; held-out set: 3 of 8 citations, 7 of 10 key facts. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalOpinionTests, opinion set v1 in the iOS 27 Simulator; FORK_CHANGES 2.11.29]`
- **[Legal]** **A Greek summary of one imported statute.** The statute is noted article by article within the phone's 4,096-token window, the list of main provisions is built from those notes with one bullet an article, the model writes only the opening, and sentences with an unsupported figure or article are dropped before translation. A document without articles, such as a ministerial decision, is summarized by parts. Law 5278/2026: 5 of 6 key provisions and 3 of 3 articles; decision 8934/2026: 0 of 5 held out (no articles), 4 of 5 after the parts were added. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalSummaryTests, summary set v1 in the iOS 27 Simulator; FORK_CHANGES 2.11.28]`
- **[Legal]** **Questions over a library of five labour-law documents.** A question that names a law of the library is searched in that law's document; a document's title page and contents no longer outrank its articles; the extractive shortcut no longer replaces a Greek legal answer. 14 of 24 development questions (13 at the start), 6 of 12 held out. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalPipelineTests, library set v1 in the iOS 27 Simulator; FORK_CHANGES 2.11.27]`
- **[Legal]** **Labour contract templates and a router that picks one.** Ten Greek labour contract templates (indefinite and fixed-term, part-time, rotating, on-call, telework, apprenticeship, internship, temporary agency, urgent needs) ship with the app. A request in Greek or English is matched to one of them, or to none, by Apple's on-device model choosing from the templates' descriptions, with keywords as the fallback. 43 of 44 development prompts and 20 of 22 held-out prompts are routed correctly. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: LabourContractTemplateTests, routing set v1 in the iOS 27 Simulator]`

- **[Documentation]** **Experiments record for the thesis.** Every evaluation run's result lines are kept in the repository (`Docs/Thesis/experiments/runs/`, collected by `scripts/thesis/collect_run_results.py`; EU holdout counts only), and `Docs/Thesis/EXPERIMENTS.md` gives each experiment's hypothesis, runs and outcome, including those not supported and changes dropped. The EU holdout, run again after the PoC work, is unchanged (12 of 12 graded right, 3 of 3 refused in each condition). Graded by hand by the owner (final): 13, 13 and 12 of 13 right (English; Greek passages with English questions; all Greek) and 3 of 3 refused in each, against 13, 12 and 13 of 13 on 2026-10-29. `[evidence_level: measured, confidence: high; evidence_source: holdout_fork4, regrade_holdout.py, owner's hand grading (not blind); FORK_CHANGES 2.11.31]`

### Fixed
- **[Legal]** **Summaries and opinions survive model errors, and Stop works between their steps.** Each model call of a summary or opinion is retried with a shorter text when it is too long for the on-device window, retried once when the model is busy, and skipped when the model declines (a summary keeps its other articles, an opinion its other documents; a declined conclusion is refused with a Greek reason). Stop now ends a summary or opinion before its next model call: in the Mac app it went idle 0.3 s after Stop, with no further pass in the log. The OS 27 error types now get their specific messages instead of a generic one. Results unchanged when nothing fails (opinion dev identical). `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: GreekLegalModelCallTests (stand-in model), dev runs in the iOS 27 Simulator (EXPERIMENTS E16), Mac app; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (seventh entry)]`
- **[Ingestion]** **No translation placeholders in passages or answers.** The translator mangles the placeholders that protect legal identifiers (`__LEX_005___`, `LEX_004___`, `__EX_ENT_003_`), and 7% of stored English passages kept them; the model copied them and a Greek opinion showed «__ΕΧΝ.». Mangled forms are now put back as their identifiers, what is left is removed, and passages already stored are repaired when read, without a re-import (450 of 8,022 passages in the Simulator → 0). Library Q&A development set 13 → 15 of 24; opinion development set 13 of 20 key facts (15 before, two phrasings); opinion test set 8 of 10 key facts, 4 of 8 citations. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalEntityMaskerTests, dev and test runs in the iOS 27 Simulator (EXPERIMENTS E15), Mac app; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (sixth entry)]`
- **[Legal]** **An opinion reads the whole article that amends the Labour Code.** When the question reaches an article of a later law that amends or adds a Code article, the model reads that whole article in order instead of its three best passages, so it sees the amended paragraphs the article restates at its end; the passages are no longer numbered, since the model cited a passage number as the article. Opinion development set: key facts 11 → 15 of 20, citations 10 of 12 as before, nothing dropped by the article check; 5–15 s longer. The overtime question of the set now concludes "up to four hours a day, 150 a year, plus 40%, and the employee may refuse". The README's opinion example is now that question, since its shorter form still retrieves the Code's sixth-day articles. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalOpinionTests, opinion dev split in the iOS 27 Simulator (EXPERIMENTS E14), Mac app; test split not run; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (fifth entry)]`
- **[UI]** **Three display bugs seen in the Mac app.** A legal opinion keeps a document name with spaces whole in its Greek text («ΑΤΟΜΙΚΟ ΕΡΓΑΤΙΚΟ ΔΙΚΑΙΟ.pdf» had become «ΔΙΚΑΙΟ.pdf»); the contract templates' signatures are two lines instead of a table the chat showed as raw `|---|`; while a summary, opinion or template runs, the bar no longer shows the previous answer's confidence and time, and afterwards it shows the capability's own time. The README's summary example reads «Συνόψισε». When an excerpt and the whole law are both imported, a summary takes the whole law (the one with more passages). `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: GreekLegalOpinionTests, LabourContractTemplateTests, Mac app on M3; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (fourth entry)]`
- **[Legal]** **A chat summary finds its document by the statute it is, not by its filename.** «σύνοψη του ν. 5278/2026» finds Law 5278/2026 whatever its file is called, using the statute each document's title page names at import; the filename is used only for documents imported before that, with whole numbers instead of substrings. A statute the library doesn't hold gets "not in the library" and the list of documents instead of another document's summary, even when the library holds one document. The list and the summary heading name the statute («ν. 5278/2026»). `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: GreekLegalChatRouterTests; not yet checked in the Mac app; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (third entry)]`
- **[Legal]** **Capability failures and refusals read as messages, not errors.** A failed summary, opinion or template request shows a fixed Greek or English message (model not ready or unavailable, text too long for the on-device model, request declined, model busy, translation models missing), never the system's error text; the model's availability is checked first. A refused opinion gives its reason in Greek, and in the Greek Legal AI sheet shows it as a notice instead of an empty card. A summary or opinion shows the "generating" stage instead of "embedding", a request stopped meanwhile is not answered, and its details show its real duration without "0 tokens". English requests get English headers and messages. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalChatRouterTests; not yet checked in the Mac app; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (second entry)]`
- **[Tests]** **`AnswerUsageTallyTests.testTheDetailsLine` passes in any region.** It expected "1,100" and failed on a Mac set to the Greece region ("1.100"). `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: AnswerUsageTallyTests]`
- **[Ingestion]** **A Greek PDF whose font maps to the wrong letters is now read with PaddleOCR.** Vision cannot check a Greek text layer, so through 13 Nov every Greek text layer was kept unchecked; a Word export of lecture notes was stored as unreadable text and every question about it was refused. - **[Legal]** **Greek wording of statute summaries, legal opinions and template answers; a decision's figures kept; foreign law named by adjective refused.** An opinion over the minimum-wage decision left out the €920 amount: the model cited the decision, which has no articles, as "Article 1", and the article check dropped the sentence. - **[Ingestion]** **Greek Gazette pages lost article headings at import.** Word positions were taken from the normalized page text, which a Greek keraia ("Α΄") lengthens, so every later word got another word's position and headings merged into the lines around them; Law 5278/2026 was stored with 8 of its 16 articles. Law 5278 is stored with all 16 articles; the Labour Code development set is unchanged (38 of 46). `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: DocumentProcessorTests testSpatialOrdering_KeraiaDoesNotShiftLaterWords and testIsSingleColumn_MedianLineWidthDecides; stored chunks audited after reimport; FORK_CHANGES 2.11.27]`

### Changed
- **[Legal]** **The chat recognises capability requests without accents and stops sending ordinary questions to them.** Rules match the prompt with accents and case removed, so «συνοψη του ν. 5278/2026» and «ΘΕΛΩ ΓΝΩΜΟΔΟΤΗΣΗ» work. Questions such as «Τι ισχύει για τη σύμβαση εργασίας για ορισμένο χρόνο;» or "In your opinion…" are answered instead of opening a template or an opinion, and a summary needs a document or statute. A described hiring arrangement («Θέλω να πάρω μια φοιτήτρια για τέσσερις ώρες…») now reaches the template router, and goes on to Q&A when no template fits. Template requests of the routing set recognised from the chat: 0 → 38 of 40 (dev), 13 of 20 (test, once); no Labour Code, library or opinion question is sent to a template or summary. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalChatRouterTests, EXPERIMENTS E13; FORK_CHANGES 14 Nov 2026 (second entry)]`
- **[UI]** **New app icon.** A drawn apple on an orange tile, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and the Live Activity; the old light-bulb dark and tinted variants are removed. `[evidence_level: build_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: actool for iOS and macOS, iOS test build]`
- **[General]** **The app is renamed Firiki.** Folders, targets, schemes, modules and types `OpenIntelligence*` are now `Firiki*` (engine `FirikiEngine`), bundle IDs `com.alagraf.firiki…`, app group `group.com.alagraf.firiki`, links `firiki://`, environment variables `FIRIKI_*`. The original app is credited in About, the README, `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Libraries imported before the rename must be imported again. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: high; evidence_source: iOS test build and macOS build succeeded, 20 Greek suites 119 tests passed, full suite 1,288 tests with only the known Greek-region format failure; FORK_CHANGES 13 Nov 2026 (second entry)]`
- **[RAG]** **A question about a statute the library doesn't hold is refused.** Each imported Greek statute now records which statute it is, read from its title page (the Labour Code, Law 5239/2025, the Constitution, …). A question that names a law, decree, decision or the Constitution that no retrieved passage comes from or cites is refused before the model answers, instead of being answered from another statute's article with the same number. The statutory isolation check also counts a passage's own statute. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalVerificationTests, Constitution set 8/10 and Labour dev 38/46 in the iOS 27 Simulator]`
- **[Ingestion]** **Tables in Greek statutes keep one row a line.** The chunker joined a paragraph's lines, so a table such as Article 333's severance scale became one run-on sentence that the translator rendered as "16 years completed and more than 12 months compensation". Table rows now stay on their own lines through chunking, cutting and translation, and a row PDFKit reads as two lines ("10 έτη συμπληρωμένα" / "6 μηνών αποζημίωση") is put back on one. Labour dev: 36 and 35 of 46 (35 before); the severance question now gets the right figure. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalStructureChunkerTests, FragmentTranslationTests, labour dev runs 15–16 in the iOS 27 Simulator]`
- **[RAG]** **An off-topic figure no longer refuses a correct Greek legal answer.** When the numeric check is the only failed check, sentences that state a figure and share no topic word with the question are dropped and the rest is verified again; it is shown only if every check passes. An unsupported figure on the question's own topic is still refused. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekLegalVerificationTests; the dev runs didn't trigger it]`
- **[Ingestion]** **Greek OCR reads tilted scans.** PaddleOCR's text boxes now follow each line's angle instead of being axis-aligned: on a page tilted by 1.5° a straight box was almost twice a line's height and took in the lines above and below, so the recogniser returned fragments. Lines that rise by less than a quarter of their thickness keep the exact straight crop, so clean pages read as before. Simulated scans in the OCR benchmark: 48% → 96% of the words (legal identifiers 61% → 92%), whole scanned-PDF import 62% → 98%, at 7.0 s per scan page against 6.6 s for a clean page. On 13 other pages with random tilts up to 2.5° (`scripts/thesis/ocr_tilt_check.py`; the threshold was set on the benchmark): 46% → 88% of the words, the same as untilted. `[evidence_level: test_verified, confidence: medium; evidence_source: GreekOCRBenchmarkTests and GreekOCRTests in the iOS 27 Simulator, ocr_tilt_check.py; simulated scans, not real ones]`
